1. Why are the variables declared as private?

The variables are declared as private to protect the data from direct access
outside the Student class. This is an example of encapsulation.

2. What happens if the variables are declared public?

If the variables are public, other classes can directly access and modify them.
This can reduce data security and control.

3. Why do we use getters and setters?

Getters are used to read private data, while setters are used to modify private
data in a controlled way.
